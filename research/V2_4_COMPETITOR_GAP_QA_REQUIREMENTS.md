# V2.4 COMPETITOR GAP & QA REQUIREMENTS CHECKPOINT

Date: 24.09.2026

## Research refresh
Fresh 2026 market evidence was reviewed across FinancialAha, Tiller, Analysistabs, Finta, Sheets and Cells, and other current template offerings.

Observed recurring expectations:
- fast setup and clear onboarding;
- dashboard KPIs and period selection;
- planned-vs-actual analysis;
- savings/debt/net-worth coverage;
- sample data or worked examples;
- mobile usability;
- clear input/calculated separation;
- print-ready output;
- compatibility transparency;
- one-time pricing as an alternative to subscriptions.

## Product gap actions
The research is converted into QA requirements, not copied content:
1. Verify Start Here/Quick Start gets a new user to first useful result quickly.
2. Verify sample/example state is understandable without exposing private data.
3. Verify blank-state formulas remain zero-safe.
4. Verify dashboard period/month/year consistency across connected sheets.
5. Verify input cells are visually distinguishable from calculated cells.
6. Verify printable pages remain readable in Letter output.
7. Verify multilingual labels remain consistent for all user-facing core terms.
8. Verify no bank connection, financial guarantee, or investment recommendation claims.

## Competitive positioning boundary
The product remains a manual, privacy-oriented, no-bank-connection personal-finance organization toolkit. It does not attempt to imitate automated bank-sync subscriptions.
