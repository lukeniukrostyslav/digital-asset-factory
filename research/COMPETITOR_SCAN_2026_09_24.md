# COMPETITOR SCAN — 24.09.2026

Fresh market scan focused on personal-finance spreadsheet products.

## Observed market patterns

Current 2026 sources show several recurring product patterns:
- basic monthly budget templates remain common;
- stronger products combine budget, expense tracking, debt, savings, net worth and annual views;
- premium spreadsheet products increasingly emphasize dashboards, charts, monthly/annual views and polished UX;
- some products add bill/recurring-payment tracking;
- some systems reduce user setup by concentrating inputs into a small number of tabs;
- privacy/local-data positioning is used by products that avoid bank connections.

Sources reviewed:
- Tiller's 2026 Excel personal-finance template review;
- FinancialAha's September 2026 spreadsheet comparison;
- Sage & Serif's 2026 budget-template review;
- Tools Without Code's September 2026 personal-finance spreadsheet architecture;
- current Etsy examples.

## Product implications

Personal Money Command Center should not remain a basic budget spreadsheet.

The production candidate now adds:
1. Setup & privacy controls
2. Bills / recurring payments tracker
3. Net Worth snapshot
4. Dashboard KPIs for bills and net worth
5. Existing Budget + Transactions + Debt + Savings + Annual Review
6. Multilingual interface: English, Spanish, Italian, Russian

## Differentiation direction

The product should emphasize:
- one-time purchase;
- no bank login;
- local workbook;
- multiple financial modules in one system;
- multilingual UI;
- mobile-aware layout;
- printable companion;
- AI prompt pack;
- clear setup guidance.

These are positioning/design directions, not guarantees of sales or superiority.
