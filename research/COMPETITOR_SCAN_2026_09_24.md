## Observed feature expectations

Current spreadsheet products commonly combine:
- budget;
- transactions/spending;
- debt;
- savings goals;
- net worth;
- dashboard/insights;
- recurring bills or cash-flow views;
- linked workbook sections rather than isolated sheets.

Tiller currently promotes budgets, spending trends, debt payoff, savings goals and net worth as a coordinated spreadsheet workflow. Its template ecosystem also includes bill payment tracking, cash-flow forecasting, transaction tracking and year-to-date comparison.

SheetFolk's 2026 bundle combines budget, sinking funds, debt payoff, net worth, savings rate and goals in one linked workbook.

Microsoft's current Excel budget planner emphasizes built-in formulas, reusable tracking and review of spending patterns.

Open-source dashboard examples show expectations around KPIs, charts, filters and data-validation dropdowns.

## Product implication

Personal Money Command Center should not remain a bare budget table.

Target experience:
**one workbook -> connected financial data -> multiple views -> clear dashboard -> guided setup -> recurring bills -> debt -> savings -> net worth -> annual review.**

## Differentiation direction

1. Multilingual interface: English / Spanish / Italian / Russian.
2. No bank credentials or account connection required.
3. Beginner-first Start Here flow.
4. Explicit privacy boundaries.
5. Connected workbook rather than unrelated templates.
6. Mobile-readable layout.
7. One-time purchase positioning.
8. Printable companion materials.
9. AI Prompt Pack for organization/review, without financial-advice claims.
10. Versioned product with checkpoints and QA.

## Competitive caution

Do not claim factual market superiority. We will optimize measurable criteria: completeness, clarity, UX, formula reliability, mobile compatibility, visual quality and QA coverage.
