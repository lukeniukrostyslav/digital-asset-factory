# V2.16 — CURRENT COMPETITOR BENCHMARK

Date: 24.09.2026

## Fresh market observations

1. Analysistabs currently sells a Personal Budget Planner & Tracker with a 12-month plan, transaction log, live dashboard, period selector, Sample + Blank files and one-time pricing. The current page shows $19 promotional pricing, while its broader template catalog lists the same product at $29.
2. Sheets & Cells currently offers a free Excel budget tracker with a 12-month workbook, dashboard, category tracking, savings-goal projections, formula-only design and print-ready output.
3. Finta's August 2026 template includes remaining budget, spending, income, savings rate, budget status, top categories and plan-vs-actual, with optional account sync.
4. Tiller's 2026 Foundation Template covers spending trends, transactions, categories, monthly/yearly budgets, accounts and balances, with linked-account automation.

## Product implications

The current category baseline is now clear:
- dashboard + period selection;
- transactions feeding downstream analysis;
- planned vs actual;
- remaining budget and savings rate;
- sample + blank workbook;
- print readiness;
- formula-only/no-macro options;
- broader systems increasingly bundle debt, savings, bills, accounts and net worth.

Our product therefore keeps these baseline functions and adds a connected manual/private workflow across Budget, Transactions, Bills, Debt, Savings, Accounts, Net Worth, Cash Flow, Spending Insights and Annual Review.

We should not claim to be objectively "the best" or copy competitors. The QA goal is to be complete, reliable, clear and commercially defensible for the chosen positioning.
