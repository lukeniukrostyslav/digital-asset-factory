# MARKET & PRICING DECISION — CHECKPOINT 01

Дата: 23.09.2026

## Product

**Personal Money Command Center**

Complete personal-finance organization toolkit:
- Excel dashboard
- budget planner
- transactions tracker
- debt tracker
- savings goals
- annual review
- printable PDF
- AI prompt pack
- quick-start guide

## Market sequence

### Market 1 — United States
Primary launch market.

Reason:
- English-first product;
- strong existing marketplace demand for budget spreadsheets and finance planners;
- competitor listings show both low-cost templates and higher-priced all-in-one systems.

### Market 2 — Canada
Same English product can be offered with minimal localization.

### Market 3 — United Kingdom
English product with later UK-specific localization where useful.

### Market 4 — Australia
English product with later localization where useful.

### Market 5 — Other English-speaking markets
Expansion after the first markets have enough listing and customer data.

Important: this is a market-entry plan, not a forecast of sales.

## Competitor pricing research

Current marketplace examples observed on Etsy on 23.09.2026 include:
- simple budget spreadsheets around $1–$4;
- more complete annual/monthly budget systems around $7–$14;
- some larger all-in-one systems above $15;
- a number of listings use temporary discounts, so sale prices and original prices are not equivalent signals.

Examples:
- Ultimate Annual Budget Spreadsheet: approximately $10.38 sale price / $20.76 displayed original price.
- Biweekly Paycheck Budget Spreadsheet: approximately $13.25 sale price / $22.09 displayed original price.
- 2026 Budget Spreadsheet with debt payoff and savings goals: $7.99.
- Some larger bundles are listed above $20.

The category is competitive: Etsy currently shows 5,000+ budget planner spreadsheet items.

## Our pricing decision

### Launch price
**$9.99 USD**

This is the fixed initial list price for the first commercial version.

### Planned regular price
**$12.99 USD**

We can move to this price after the product is fully finished and the listing/product quality justify the higher positioning.

### Launch promotion
No permanent deep discount is planned.

If a launch promotion is used, it should be temporary and clearly separated from the normal list price.

## Positioning

We do NOT compete primarily as a $1–$3 basic spreadsheet.

The product is positioned as an **all-in-one personal money organization toolkit**.

The higher value comes from the complete system:
dashboard + budget + transactions + debt + savings + annual review + printable version + AI prompts + quick-start guide.

## Sales channels

## Pricing caveat

Prices are a commercial hypothesis based on observed competitor pricing, not a guarantee of conversion or revenue. Actual pricing may be revised after real customer data.
