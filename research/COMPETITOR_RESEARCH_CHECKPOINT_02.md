# COMPETITOR_RESEARCH_CHECKPOINT_02

Дата: 24.09.2026

## Fresh market findings

Current 2026 competitor/reference research shows that stronger spreadsheet finance systems increasingly connect:
- budget;
- transactions;
- spending analysis;
- debt;
- savings goals;
- bills;
- account balances;
- net worth;
- cash flow;
- dashboards/insights.

Tiller's current Foundation and template ecosystem explicitly covers spending trends, transactions, monthly/yearly budgets, balances, accounts, debt payoff, savings goals and net worth. Tiller also describes coordinated systems where multiple views draw from the same underlying data.

A newer September 2026 competitor example, Tools Without Code, positions a nine-tab personal finance system around Setup, Transaction Log, Dashboard, Monthly Budget, Bill Calendar, Savings Goals, Debt Tracker, Net Worth and Annual View, with only two input tabs.

## Product response

The Personal Money Command Center is therefore being developed as a connected system rather than a collection of unrelated spreadsheets.

Added in system pass:
- Accounts;
- Spending Insights;
- Cash Flow;
- Dashboard system insight KPIs;
- existing Budget, Transactions, Bills, Debt Tracker, Savings Goals, Net Worth and Annual Review remain connected components.

## Product differentiation direction

The product should emphasize:
1. one workbook;
2. clear beginner onboarding;
3. four-language interface;
4. no bank credentials or account connection required;
5. privacy-first manual data ownership;
6. connected views;
7. mobile-readable UX;
8. practical recurring-bill and net-worth tracking;
9. clear dashboards without pretending to provide financial advice.

## Research limitation

Competitor research informs product requirements and UX priorities. It does not establish that this product will outsell any competitor or guarantee commercial results.
