# COMPETITOR RESEARCH CHECKPOINT 04

Date: 24.09.2026

## Fresh market signals

A current 2026 comparison of personal-finance spreadsheets evaluates setup ease, feature depth, visual design, customization, mobile usability and value. Current connected-template examples emphasize a single dashboard fed by transactions/setup data, while paid Excel bundles increasingly combine budget, sinking funds, debt, net worth, savings goals and annual views.

## Design/product implications adopted

1. The workbook must behave as one connected system rather than a collection of independent tabs.
2. The selected review month/year must drive month-sensitive views.
3. Dashboard KPIs must have unambiguous labels and source links.
4. Annual Review must derive from the same transaction ledger rather than manual duplicate entry.
5. Spending Insights must actually respect the selected month; otherwise its label would be misleading.
6. Mobile-safe formulas remain preferable to unnecessarily advanced Excel-only functions.
7. The product should remain manual-entry and privacy-oriented rather than requiring bank credentials or subscriptions.

## v1.8 changes

- Dashboard KPI hierarchy clarified.
- Budget Actual column connected to selected month/year Transactions.
- Spending Insights changed from all-time totals to selected-month totals.
- Annual Review connected to monthly Transactions for income, expenses, net savings and debt-payment category.
- Bills dashboard formulas moved to a workbook month list for cross-engine compatibility.
- Cash Flow month labels preserved through the multilingual translation layer.
- Formula QA repeated through LibreOffice with synthetic multi-month data.

## Competitive boundary

Research is used to define category expectations and QA criteria only. No competitor text, branding, artwork or proprietary implementation is copied.
