## 12. Cross-market pain-point matrix

### Повторяющиеся положительные факторы

1. Ready-made structure
2. Easy navigation
3. Simple/straightforward
4. Beautiful/aesthetic
5. Customizable
6. All-in-one coverage
7. Time saved versus building from scratch
8. Beginner-friendly
9. Tutorials/videos
10. Clear organization

### Повторяющиеся проблемы

1. Too many pages / potential overwhelm
2. Cluttered/confusing layouts
3. Widgets can break or become unavailable
4. Learning curve for Notion beginners
5. Buyer must customize too much
6. Desktop/mobile presentation differences
7. Freemium/unlocked-page expectations
8. External integrations create maintenance burden
9. Some templates are visually attractive but bland in utility
10. Complex systems need better onboarding

## 13. Product implications

### Must have

- clean Home dashboard;
- Today view;
- This Week;
- goals;
- projects;
- tasks;
- habits;
- review system;
- quick capture;
- clear navigation;
- beginner Quick Start;
- sample workspace;
- blank workspace;
- mobile-first layout;
- minimal external dependencies.

### Should have

- AI Assistant Hub;
- 90-day planning;
- life areas;
- monthly/quarterly review;
- printable review pages;
- short video/tutorial links;
- visual theme options.

### Avoid in V1

- large number of third-party widgets;
- fragile external integrations;
- excessive decorative pages;
- too many databases exposed on Home;
- complex automation that needs maintenance;
- medical/health outcome claims;
- copying competitor terminology/design.

## 14. Core product principle

**"Simple enough to start today; deep enough to keep using."**

The system should expose only the actions needed now and keep advanced modules behind navigation.
