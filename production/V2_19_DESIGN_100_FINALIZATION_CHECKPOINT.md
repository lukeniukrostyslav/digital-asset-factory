## Design completion criteria

The product design system is now treated as complete for v1.0:
- consistent professional header hierarchy across workbook;
- dashboard KPI hierarchy;
- visible distinction between input and calculated cells;
- mobile-readable labels and widths;
- frozen panes where useful;
- hidden gridlines;
- print-fit configuration;
- chart on Budget for Planned vs Actual;
- multilingual labels for English, Russian, Italian and Spanish;
- Start Here onboarding;
- blank and sample visual consistency;
- no obvious clipping/overlap in rendered all-sheet review;
- printable PDF layouts checked;
- no macros/external links used for core design or calculations.
