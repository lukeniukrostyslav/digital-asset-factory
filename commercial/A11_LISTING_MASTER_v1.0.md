## FAQ

### Does this connect to my bank?
No. The core workbook uses manual data entry and does not require bank credentials or bank connections.

### Is a sample included?
Yes. A Sample workbook is included alongside the Blank workbook.

### Is this Excel?
Yes. The primary workbook format is .xlsx.

### Are macros required?
No. Core calculations do not require VBA/macros.

### Can I customize categories?
Yes. The workbook is designed for personal customization.

### Is this financial advice?
No. It is an organizational and tracking toolkit.

### What languages are supported?
The workbook includes English, Russian, Italian and Spanish language support.
